Our Story

Snip Snip was founded by Marcie Stuward, a visionary 34-year-old driven by a passion for exquisite hair artistry and unparalleled customer service. Marcie envisioned a salon experience that transcended the ordinary, where every client felt seen, heard, and utterly transformed.

With this dream, she established Snip Snip in the heart of Auckland CBD. From its inception, the salon has been dedicated to offering a premium sanctuary for hair care, providing everything from intricate, personalised haircuts and professional-grade colouring to luxurious perms and revitalizing treatments. Marcie's commitment to exceptional quality and an extremely personalized approach is the cornerstone of Snip Snip, justifying its premium price tag and cementing its reputation as a haven for bespoke beauty.
